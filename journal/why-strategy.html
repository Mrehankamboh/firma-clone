Why Strategy Should Always Come Before Design

It’s easy to jump straight into colors, logos, and layouts. But without a clear strategy, even the best design won’t connect. Before anything is created, the foundation must be set: who it’s for, what it should change, and the one idea the work has to carry.

At Firma, strategy is the brief we design against. It decides the words, the system, and what we leave out. Decoration can be charming. A story people remember is the point.
